---
name: Product announcement wording
description: User-approved timing and distinct labels for new products versus restocked products.
---

Wait until an active product has stock before announcing it. The user chose “Wait until stocked.”

**Why:** An empty product must not be advertised as ready to buy.

Use “New product” for the first stocked announcement and “Added stock” for subsequent inventory additions to an existing product.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested different wording rather than the same “New in stock” heading for both events.

**How to apply:** Remember the distinction across restarts and sold-out periods; becoming available again does not make an existing product new.
